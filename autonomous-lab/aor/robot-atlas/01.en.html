MicroDuck

Two legs, a neck and a head: understanding 14 body joints.

MicroDuck has five rotary joints in each leg and four in its neck and head. Each leg has three hip rotations, a knee and an ankle. Left and right refer to the robot’s own sides. A separate free joint records the whole body’s position and orientation. It is not a fifteenth motor: the local model has njnt=15 and nu=14.

The picture uses the local Pollen Robotics model’s STAND keyframe. It was posed and rendered offscreen, without a walking policy or physics time steps. Callouts identify representative body locations, not precise visible joint centres. This static pose does not prove stable standing. Existing v3 route walking still uses disclosed planar force and yaw assistance.

The source is Pollen Robotics microduck_rl, local commit 29e887ecfbf5d37144759e5a9f8a176dfb83d547. The displayed model loads scene.xml → robot_groundcontact.xml, rather than the older browser collision model. The local root licence is Apache-2.0. Detailed joint names and local verification identifiers are retained in the Chinese note.
