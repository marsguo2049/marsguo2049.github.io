ABOUT THE DEMOS

About the demos

This site uses existing CarlaAir, Isaac and MuJoCo recordings. Clips are excerpts at their original speed, with some technical overlays cropped or simplified. Generated imagery has not replaced experiments, and robot motion has not been altered.

These demos illustrate research scenarios and possibilities. Riding proxies, walking assistance and integration status are explained alongside each scene. A simulation demo does not establish hardware capability or an overall success rate.

Robot models and control use assets from the relevant open-source projects or simulation platforms. This site does not maintain a separate robot specification database; the introductory notes are not specifications for a particular device.
