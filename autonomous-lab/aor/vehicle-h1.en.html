Vehicle–H1 / Scenario notes

When is it worth waiting for a partner?

Nearby tasks can be reached on foot. For longer distances, a mobile platform can help—but waiting and transfers take time too.

The question is how to work together.

Choosing how to collaborate means considering both distance and the cost of connecting actions. A partner’s location matters as much as the help it can provide.

What does this demo show?

An Isaac simulation. Walking uses an existing robot policy; riding uses a position-following proxy. Physical boarding, alighting and riding have not been validated.

These excerpts come from existing simulation demos, with simplified overlays and unchanged playback speed. They explain the scenario; they are not hardware tests or general performance claims.
