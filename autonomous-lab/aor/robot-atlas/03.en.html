ILLUSTRATED NOTES / 03

Stretch 2 / 3

A mobile base, lift and telescoping arm divide the work of moving.

Stretch combines wheels, a lift, a segmented arm, a wrist and a gripper, including passive and coupled joints. The local Stretch 3 has 20 scalar body joints, 21 joint elements, 37 bodies and 10 control channels. Stretch 2 has 17, 18, 26 and 8, respectively. Body counts exclude the world body and are not counts of actual mechanical parts.

Stretch 3 is the main image, with Stretch 2 shown separately below. The pose was set in memory and evaluated with mj_forward to show the structure. It is a static illustration, not a completed control motion.

Early v0 transport used a tray and a switchable weld. The v3 demonstration uses body contact to grasp Duck. Duck walking remains assisted, and the scripted physical demonstration is separate from AOR optimization integration. The Chinese card preserves model versions, licences and these distinctions.
