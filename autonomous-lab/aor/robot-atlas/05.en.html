ILLUSTRATED NOTES / 05

Two different carrier mechanisms

One moves through planar actuators; the other through wheel–ground contact.

The old carrier on the left has decorative wheels and three planar actuators for X, Y and yaw. The chassis on the right has a free base and two hinged wheels with velocity servos; wheel–ground contact drives the body. An underside view reveals the wheels and spherical supports.

The right-hand model is the AOR-013 simplified research chassis, not an exact OOMWOO reproduction. The illustrated frozen model has no tray, ramp or external assistance. This describes its configuration; the image itself is a static render.

The old carrier contributes 3/3/3 to nq/nv/nu; the new chassis contributes 9/8/2. The full frozen Duck-plus-chassis model is 30/28/16. These are different statistical objects and must remain separate.

Some isolated physical segments have been demonstrated under their stated configurations. The complete continuous transport chain has not passed. Separate standing or approach tests cannot be joined into a success claim for boarding, transport, alighting and a subsequent Stretch grasp.
