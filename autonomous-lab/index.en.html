The path from simulation to collaboration＋
  1. Build a simulation world we can observe.
  2. Give an individual agent the ability to act.
  3. Connect observations, policies and control.
  4. Coordinate tasks across several agents.
  5. Combine the strengths of different robots.
  6. Use operations research to study assignment, routing and timing.

This is a research path, not a claim that every stage is complete. See each project for its actual progress.

Application ideas being explored＋

Multimodal travel in an open world

Study routes and transfers between walking, vehicles and other modes in virtual worlds such as GTA.

A robotic kitchen

Study how robots could share tasks and schedule actions once navigation and manipulation are available.

Coordinated emergency response

A long-term vision of vehicles, drones, robots and boats contributing their different strengths.

These are research ideas, not claims of complete execution capability.
