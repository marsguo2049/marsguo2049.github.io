ILLUSTRATED NOTES / 02

One robot, or a whole scene?

Adding a ball lengthens the state tables, not the robot’s joint list.

A simulation records every movable object in the scene. A ball has no controller, but still needs a position, orientation and velocity. The local free ball adds 7 qpos + 6 qvel and no ctrl. Contact makes it move; it does not need a motor command.

Objects occupy separate segments of the scene’s state arrays and can still use separate controllers. mj_step advances the whole scene and calculates contact and forces together. A larger scene state does not mean Duck has acquired more joints.

The three local compiled scenes have nq/nv/nu of 21/20/14, 28/26/14 and 62/58/24. The third contains Stretch 3, Duck and two free balls. Its counts are not standalone robot specifications. Fixed tables add no state dimensions here; movable tables would need separate accounting. The Stretch thumbnail is a local static render; objects are not at a common scale.
