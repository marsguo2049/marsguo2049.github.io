ILLUSTRATED NOTES / 04

H1 / Nova Carter

Walking and wheel driving are physical; the current ride uses a pose proxy.

For H1, a desired body velocity feeds a policy that produces joint actions. For Carter, forward speed and turning rate become left and right wheel speeds. Both have physical motion in the existing scene.

The current H1 ride updates the robot’s pose in code. Seeing H1 move with Carter therefore does not establish contact-based support or physical boarding and alighting.

These images are cropped from an existing Isaac frame, so enlarged detail is limited by the original resolution. No new Isaac capture or substitute H1 model was used. Complete USD joint, body and drive counts remain unknown. Historical imagery does not resolve asset versions or licences.
