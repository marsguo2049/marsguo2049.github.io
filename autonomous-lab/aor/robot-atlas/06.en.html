ILLUSTRATED NOTES / 06

CarlaAir ground–air collaboration

Understand the controls, coordinates and meeting signals of a vehicle and drone.

Read this scene as vehicle → control interface → coordinates → meeting and observation. A CARLA blueprint and actor identify the car; an AirSim vehicle name and flight configuration identify the drone. Counting MuJoCo joint arrays would not describe these interfaces.

Traffic Manager receives the car’s destination. AirSim flight control receives the drone’s NED target. OR decides who should go where and when to meet. These are separate responsibilities.

CARLA’s Z axis points up, while AirSim NED’s Z axis points down. Local origin offsets must also be handled: identical [x,y,z] values need not identify the same place. The Chinese card links official coordinate references and explains the assumptions behind the local conversion.

The historical frame retains its presentation-only label. Camera access is not visual inspection; logical inspection is not image recognition; READY is not physical loading or unloading. Historical synchronization evidence covers its stated movement, waiting and readiness scope, not a physical handoff.
