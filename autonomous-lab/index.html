AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS LAB · 无人实验室

What comes after
autonomy?

When vehicles can drive, robots can manipulate and drones can fly, the system-level question becomes: who should do what, when, where, with which mode and resource? This lab explores that question through simulation and operations research.

Open worlds are excellent for discovering problems; scientific simulators are better for controlled validation. The algorithm should survive both.

Lab principles

01 · MODULAR

Do not bind OR to one simulator or one robot.

02 · VERIFY

Separate visual demos from reproducible experiments.

03 · COMPARE

Always preserve a baseline and measurable metrics.

04 · COORDINATE

Use AI for individual capability; use OR where system decisions matter.
