AOR · HETEROGENEOUS ROBOT COLLABORATION

Different strengths.
Shared action.

Walking, flying, carrying.
When robots with different abilities meet,
how can they do a job better together?

AOR = Autonomous OR
OR stands for Operations Research: using mathematical methods to help make decisions. AOR studies how to assign work, plan routes and coordinate timing so different robots can work better together.

IN SIMULATION / WORKING TOGETHER

See what they can do together.

Three scenes. Three ways to help each other.
Press play. Each clip is under a minute.

01 / Truck–Drone

Different strengths, on the ground and in the air.

A vehicle moves along roads while a drone approaches targets from the air. Meeting in the right place creates an opportunity to work together.

A CarlaAir simulation of ground–air movement and rendezvous. Inspection is represented by a stable hold in this demo; visual recognition is not included.

02 / Vehicle–H1

Sometimes walk. Sometimes get a helping hand.

Nearby tasks can be reached on foot. For longer distances, a mobile platform can help—but waiting and transfers take time too.

An Isaac simulation. Walking uses an existing robot policy; riding uses a position-following proxy. Physical boarding, alighting and riding have not been validated.

03 / Stretch–MicroDuck

Help a partner reach somewhere new.

One robot can carry; the other can move on a tabletop. Stretch grasps and lifts the small robot, bringing it to a new place to work.

A MuJoCo contact-physics demonstration. MicroDuck walking includes planar assistance. The demo is scripted and has not yet been connected to AOR’s coordination optimization workflow.

THE QUESTION / RESEARCH DIRECTION

From moving alone
to working together.

My research background is in multimodal transport.
I want to bring ideas about travel modes, transfers and coordination into collaboration between robots.

Who does what? Where should they meet? When should they act? AOR uses operations research to study these choices and coordinate existing robot abilities.

This is a proposed research connection from multimodal transport to robot collaboration. City2049 is the long-term vision; AOR is one step toward it.
